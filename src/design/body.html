Sentra

An Odoo ERP with a custom car-wash module, monitored by Prometheus, Alertmanager and Grafana. When an alert fires, an SRE agent investigates with read-only tools and proposes one allowlisted fix. A human must approve it. Then it verifies the result and writes a postmortem to an append-only audit log.
